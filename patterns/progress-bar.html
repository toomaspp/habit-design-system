Progress bar

A labeled track + fill, used for per-habit completion in the Stats screen's "By habit" section.

Guidelines

  • Fill is always --color-accent-secondary — this is the one progress indicator in the app that doesn't use accent-primary (that's reserved for the ring on Home and the weekly chart's highlighted bar).
  • Label + percentage sit above the bar, never inside or overlapping it.
  • Track is --color-ink-8, full width of its container, 6px tall — don't resize per instance.
