Guidelines

  • Unlike the habit list, rows here have no gap between them — they're divided by the 1px border instead.
  • The card clips its corners (overflow: hidden) so the top/bottom rows still get the rounded radius even though they're flush against the card edge.
  • Only one settings list per screen — for multiple groups, use separate cards with their own section label, not one long list.
