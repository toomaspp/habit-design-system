A vertical stack of Habit rows, capped with the "add a habit" ghost row.

Guidelines

  • 10px gap between rows; the ghost "add" row is always last, never first or mixed in between real habits.
  • Order habits by time of day where possible (morning → evening) rather than by completion state.
  • No pagination or scrolling truncation planned — if the list grows very long, that's a future design decision, not something this pattern currently handles.
