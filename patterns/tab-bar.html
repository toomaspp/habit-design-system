Guidelines

  • Exactly one tab is active at a time — matching the current screen, never zero or more than one.
  • Always full-width and flush to the bottom edge — this app deliberately doesn't use a floating/inset capsule style.
  • The bar itself is --color-accent-secondary on every screen; only the active tab's color/weight changes.
