Guidelines

  • Default card is --color-surface on --radius-md — this is the workhorse for stat groupings, chart containers, and lists.
  • The Hero variant (.card--hero) is reserved for exactly one thing per screen at most — the single "most important thing right now" callout (e.g. today's progress). Using it more than once per screen defeats the point.
  • Cards don't nest inside each other — a card's content is rows/text/charts, never another card.
